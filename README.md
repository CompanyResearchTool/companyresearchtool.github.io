# Company Research Tool

An independent, free educational resource designed to help transitioning U.S. military personnel research potential civilian employers.

## How It Works

1. Visit https://companyresearchtool.github.io/
2. Copy the Company Research Tool instructions.
3. Open ChatGPT and paste the instructions.
4. Enter the company you wish to research.
5. Review the results and generate your cinematic Company Research Card.

## Research Framework

The tool covers six areas:

1. Industry
2. Size
3. Location
4. Stage
5. Brand
6. Culture

The seventh section, **Bottom Line & Best Fit**, summarizes the findings and identifies relevant civilian career fields.

## Cost

The website is free to access. Students use their own ChatGPT accounts, subject to their individual feature and usage limits.

## Purpose

Help transitioning service members make informed decisions about civilian employers through structured, evidence-based company research.
